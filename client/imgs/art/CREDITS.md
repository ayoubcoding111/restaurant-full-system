## AI-generated mood images

`ai-oven-fire-*`, `ai-dough-*`, `ai-ingredients-*` and `ai-into-oven-*` were generated with
Canva's image generator for this site (October 2026) and exported from the Canva design
"Delicious pizzeria mood board". They are atmosphere only and are never presented as a
specific dish from the menu.
